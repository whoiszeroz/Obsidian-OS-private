# 🎬 Production Guide
← [[Social Media]]

## How to use it
1. Read the plan + rules
2. **Film:** follow each video's scene table, read the script out loud for the voice-over
3. **Edit + export:** checklist below
4. **Upload:** copy-paste from each video's upload kit

## 📏 Rules for all videos
- **Camera:** natural light, close-ups mixed with wide outdoor shots, clean text
- **Editing:** strong first second, calm pacing, simple cuts, quiet sound effects
- **Start fast:** open on my face. No logo intro.
- **Be real:** only show things I'm actually doing. Don't fake finished projects.
- **Privacy:** check every shot (list below)

## ⚙️ Upload settings (same for all)
| | |
|---|---|

## ✅ Checklists (for every video)
*Plain lists so they don't flood your task list. Copy into a content note if you want to tick them.*

**1. Before you film**
- Phone/camera charged, enough storage, lens cleaned
- Tripod or something stable for the camera
- 1–2 locations picked; light and weather checked
- Editing app installed, project set to 16:9

**2. While you film**
- Wide, medium and close-up shots
- Extra details + some background sound
- Voice-over somewhere quiet, several takes

**3. Privacy check**
- Mirrors, windows, shiny surfaces: no reflections of me or my home
- No university names, street signs, addresses or number plates
- No laptop/phone screens showing notifications, documents or personal info
- Nothing that shows my daily routine or where I am right now

**4. Export**
- 1920 × 1080, MP4 (H.264 video, AAC audio)
- Same frame rate as the footage (usually 30 or 60 fps)
- Voice clearly louder than the music
- Captions spelled right and easy to read on a phone
- No text near the bottom or right edge (app buttons cover it)
- Music + sound effects allowed on each platform

**5. Upload**
- Watch the whole export once, start to finish
- Title, description, cover, audience, playlist all set
- Check visibility before making it public
- Watch the published version: sound + picture OK?
- Post the update on X (Instagram + TikTok later)
- Save raw footage, final video, edit project
- A few days later: views, watch time, comments

> [!success] The standard
> **Start before it's perfect. Show real work. Keep it clear. Protect your privacy. Publish, learn, improve.**
