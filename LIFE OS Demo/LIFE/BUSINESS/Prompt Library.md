# 📚 Prompt Library
← [[Claude]] · [[Business]]

Copy a prompt into Claudian or the Claude app. Replace the parts in [brackets].

## 1. Research a business option
> Research **[online tutoring]** for me as a side business next to my studies. Fill in `Business/Research/[Tutoring].md`: customers & prices, a concrete first-customer plan for the next 30 days, what I need to learn or build, legal setup (registration, taxes). Tick the checklist items you've covered, add sources, and suggest scores (0–10) for fit, fast money, real wealth and first customer in 30 days.

## 2. Process my inbox
> Process my Business inbox. For each file in `Business/Inbox`: read it, put the information into the right note (money → Expense Log, exams → Uni course notes, business info → the matching research note), then move the file to `Business/Inbox/Done`. Tell me what you filed where.

## 3. Log expenses from a receipt or bank statement
> Add these expenses to my Expense Log, using my categories from Budget Settings. Ask me if a category isn't clear: [paste lines or attach the receipt]

## 4. Exams from a PDF
> Here's my timetable / course list. For each course that's not in `Uni` yet, tell me to import the .ics on the Uni page, then fill in ECTS and the exam date on each course page: [attach PDF or paste the list]

## 5. Plan content
> Using my Production Guide and Content Ideas, plan my next [4] pieces of content: create one note each in `Social Media/Content` from the Content Template with hook, script, shots and upload kit, with publish dates spread over the next [2 weeks].

## 6. Weekly summary
> Read my vault (habits, workouts, money this week, tasks done, content board) and write me a short weekly review: what went well, what slipped, and the ONE thing to focus on next week.

## 7. Compare two things
> Compare [option A] and [option B] for me with a table and a clear recommendation, and save it as a note in [folder].
